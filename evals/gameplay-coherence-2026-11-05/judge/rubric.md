These authored cases calibrate interpretation, not actual engine/rendering skill. They were prepared with the instruction update; they are not held-out evidence. Do not turn a future answer's use of particular words into a pass. Evaluate reasoning, preservation and claimed evidence. Keep reviewer disagreements and unsupported conclusions visible.

Record per case: judgment, basis in supplied evidence, unknown cause/intent, preservation, actionable next step, unsupported claims, and prohibited scope expansion. A proposed test is not an executed test. Source/state correctness, rendered expression and human preference are distinct evidence levels.

If comparing conditions, report supported findings, false positives, harmful repairs, unsupported claims and usable proposals separately. Do not sum them into a slop percentage or infer general effectiveness from twelve authored prompts. This key itself is not an execution result.
