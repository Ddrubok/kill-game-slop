# Real-game review and reference-routing pilot — 2026-11-09

[한국어](protocol.ko.md)

Freeze the packet, runtime, rubric and prompts before launch. Five fresh gpt-6-sol medium sessions use the shared Astra runner with automatic skill instructions/apps/search disabled: generic, brief and full art/content reviews, plus full-skill copy and whole-game tasks. All receive the identical packet; only full receives the runtime, only brief receives the preservation paragraph. Art task wording is otherwise identical. No response or judge key is delivered. Each workspace has its own Git root; restrictions are instructions, not OS isolation.

The new real-game material is ChessAlkkkaegi cosmetics: theme/state variants, actual effective copy and source. Promotion is an explicitly previously reviewed defect control, with historical evidence. This is one frozen game, not unseen games or natural play. No fresh novel art defect is asserted. An art bible, narrative branches, clips, audio and late-game evidence are absent. Claims beyond this evidence are scored as overreach.

Record misses of the known control, false positive judgments, unnecessary required changes, preservation and uncertainty using the frozen rubric. The coordinator performs disclosed provisional AI grading after seeing answers; no blinded human grading or statistical significance is claimed. New findings outside the rubric are adjudicated separately with evidence, never silently added to the denominator.

Count actual completed-turn input, cached input, output and elapsed runner wall time. Uncached input = input minus cached; total = input plus output. Preserve raw JSONL and failures. Record observed reference reads from command traces, including partial/truncated reads; reading assertions in the final answer alone do not count. Check file scope and the exact copy edit. Different task scopes have different work, so their totals are not an instruction-overhead comparison. One run per condition is descriptive; do not claim stable savings, dollar costs or accuracy superiority. The prior Nov 6 measurements do not measure this version.

The intended routing observation is small copy → presentation only; art → art/content and relevant presentation; whole → whole-game plus relevant domain references. Additional reads are reported, not hidden or automatically treated as defects. No checklist expansion follows unless the outputs demonstrate a missing generalizable instruction.
