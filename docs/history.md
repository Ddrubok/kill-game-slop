## 2026-11-06

The [repair comparison](../evals/runs/2026-11-06-repair-comparison/report.md) tested one known promotion component, four conditions, three runs each. All conditions passed functional repair checks 3/3 after one disclosed visual adjudication. **For the 2026-11-06 version**, full-skill median total tokens decreased 51.6% and elapsed time decreased 33.2% relative to the previous full skill; uncached input increased 21.2%. Function plus observed write-scope compliance was 2/3. These are neither monetary savings nor measurements of the subsequent art/content or sampling revisions.

The [synthetic comparison](../evals/runs/2026-11-06-comparison-utf8/report.md) tested generic, brief and full instructions once each on the same 26 C/U/V/safety cases. All met the provisional AI rubric. The full condition used 2.42 times the brief condition's cumulative input tokens. This establishes no stable effectiveness or cost ranking.

The [v2 response](../evals/review-v2-2026-11-06/report.md) and [security/quality record](../evals/security-quality-2026-11-06/report.md) document checker repairs, review-data trust boundaries, scoped collection, browser request policies and exclusive evidence writes. [Maintenance commands](../evals/MAINTENANCE.md) support reruns. These check tools and evidence integrity, not overall game quality.
