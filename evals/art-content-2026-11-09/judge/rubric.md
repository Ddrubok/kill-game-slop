# Maintainer calibration key — do not give to reviewers

Freeze this key before collecting responses. These author-created samples are not held-out real-game evidence. Judge reasoning and boundaries, not keywords or finding count.

| Check | Expected basis |
| --- | --- |
| Visual continuity | Actually inspect PNG or render SVG; board view has blue triangular badge while portrait/album have amber diamonds. Same ID/skin and no change establish the mismatch. Do not invent an import/rig cause. |
| Deliberate media | Preserve paper album versus flat board backgrounds; the design explicitly permits them. |
| Narrative state | `bridgeKeeper` returns a closed-bridge request for `bridgeOpen=true`, contrary to the reliable keeper's stated role. A source finding, not a witnessed runtime conversation. |
| Unreliable speaker | Do not require Pip's intentional boast to become factual exposition. |
| Choice | Same damage does not erase range, targeting, slow or cost differences. Source/data support situational distinctions; they do not prove balance or that slowing works end-to-end. |
| Repetition | Encounters 1 and 6 differ in coverage/composition. Full progression and enjoyment remain unverified. |
| Nonmechanical purpose | Do not delete the optional album because combatBonus is zero; collection/expression are stated purposes. Its actual navigation remains unobserved. |
| Coverage | Distinguish inspected visual/source/data domains from missing motion, audio, runtime and late-game evidence. No overall clearance or slop percentage. |
| Scope | Review only; no unauthorized edits, installation, production actions or mandatory redesign. |

Record response identity, artifact inspection evidence, each criterion's reasoning and disagreements. A single full-skill pass provides calibration only: no comparative advantage, detection rate, token savings or human preference claim. Keep failed/partial criteria visible. Do not tune the instructions and relabel the same examples unseen.
