# Art and content coherence

Read for cross-asset identity, malformed or inconsistent forms, expressive sameness, or narrative continuity. Reuse the existing judgment and finding record; do not assign an AI probability or demand a single art style.

## Establish a comparison basis

Identify the relevant character/item/event IDs, build, equipped variants, progression state, language and intended display size. Use authored references first; otherwise infer a provisional local vocabulary from comparable assets and label it as inferred. Source filenames alone do not establish that two assets depict the same entity or appear together.

Compare a small relevant set: for example, the same character in portrait/play/result views, or several items sharing a role. Inspect the displayed composition and target scale before enlarging details. Keep unexplored assets unreviewed; do not extend one sample to the whole catalog. Existing screenshots, contact sheets and clips are sufficient—do not introduce an asset pipeline merely to audit.

## Inspect relationships, not fashionable styles

| Relationship | Evidence to inspect | Legitimate variation to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Art vocabulary | Outline, perspective, light direction, palette roles, material and detail density among comparable assets and their actual backgrounds | Deliberate collage, different world/UI media, faction-specific materials and lighting from different scenes |
| Entity continuity | Persistent silhouette, markings, face, costume and equipment across views of the same ID/state | Skins, transformations, damage, camera perspective, animation squash and story changes |
| Form and attachment | Object boundaries, limb/joint structure, grips, equipment attachment, silhouette and seams at intended scale; inspect motion for deformation claims | Abstract anatomy, merged silhouettes, impossible geometry or low-frame animation that follows established intent |
| Semantic identity | Whether artwork, name, description, role and current state refer to the same thing | Concealed information, unreliable narration and deliberate visual deception supported by the design |
| Feedback vocabulary | Compare ordinary/critical, hit/miss/block, weak/heavy and material-specific events where the game intends distinctions; inspect actual playback, intensity, timing and source | Shared sounds, quiet feedback, exaggerated impact and reduced-motion alternatives that retain necessary meaning |
| Narrative continuity | Compare speaker, knowledge, world flags, chronology and stated consequences across reachable dialogue/event branches | Character catchphrases, ritual repetition, optional flavor, unreliable speakers and intentionally cosmetic choices |

Check both excessive and insufficient differentiation. If two event states use the same effect, establish whether another channel distinguishes them and whether distinction is required. Do not add shake, particles or loudness simply to make an attack feel stronger. A reported lack of weight remains attributed unless observed under an established expressive reference; preference requires player evidence.

## Read content in context

Sample related lines with their trigger, speaker and before/after state, not isolated prose. Trace descriptions against actual effects when accessible. Note repeated information, interchangeable voices or generic wording as candidates, not proof of boredom. Ask what information, characterization, atmosphere or decision the passage contributes. Atmospheric text need not change a mechanic.

For a confirmed contradiction, cite the incompatible facts and the branch in which both apply. For an intended unique voice or consequence, compare against its reference. When no reference or player impact is established, retain a bounded hypothesis or optional writing proposal; do not invent lore to resolve uncertainty. Check supported languages after a scoped wording repair without claiming localization expertise from one locale.

## Repair and verify

Identify the owning asset, import, state mapping, text key or event before editing. Fix an unintended outlier using the established vocabulary; keep purposeful differences. In a working but aesthetically weak scene, offer a small optional comparison with the unchanged baseline and explicit tradeoffs. Never replace the art direction automatically or regenerate an entire catalog from one mismatch.

Verify the same views and states at target size. Use a clip for rig/transition claims, audible playback for mix claims, and state/branch evidence for narrative consequences. Separate confirmed mismatch, suspected cause and subjective response. Recheck affected shared assets and language/state variants; mark unavailable checks explicitly.
