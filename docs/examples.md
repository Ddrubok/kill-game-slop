# What changes, and what stays

These are illustrative examples, not before/after measurements from shipped games.

| Game | Before | After | Keep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arena shooter | Every panel pulses while a health alert shakes the camera | Stable panels; health feedback respects motion settings | Neon team outlines and readable danger signals |
| Cozy farm | Three celebration panels interrupt each carrot harvest | One receipt showing the actual harvest | Warm illustrations and meaningful milestone animation |
| Card battler | “Locked!” before the server accepts a changeable choice | “Sending choice…” while pending; “Choice received · change it before the timer ends” only after acknowledgement and while changes remain allowed | Server authority and the opponent's hidden information |
| Pixel roguelike | Authored ornate borders; two placeholder descriptions | Accurate descriptions | The borders, pixel font and controller focus |
| Moving melee attack | Intended grounded steps become a stationary-leg slide during attack | Repair the evidenced animation/motion mismatch | Permitted movement, hit window and authored style |
| Gliding characters | Fixed leg poses intentionally express skating | No repair required in the reviewed sequence | Deliberate abstraction and moving attacks |

The skill asks for an observed functional or expression mismatch and a basis in the game's intent before requiring a repair. A visible discontinuity can be a presentation defect without a measured input failure; appearance alone does not establish human confusion or enjoyment. Sometimes the correct fix is two sentences. Sometimes no visual change is justified.

Reviews classify decisions as confirmed defect, hypothesis, optional identity proposal or preserve. A source trace and a screenshot support different claims; neither should be presented as a player test. Optional proposals compare a bounded alternative with the current version and describe gains, losses and a success check.

Read the [calibration cases](../skills/kill-game-slop/references/examples.md) for the reasoning and verification behind these examples.
